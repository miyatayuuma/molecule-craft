# Polymer growth continuity

`src/reaction-lab-polymerization.js` remains the finite chemistry authority. After the actual finite `PolymerSample` graph has finished its existing graph presentation, the Reaction Lab viewer resolves the displayed carbon backbone and the newest continuation end from the sample's real atom origins and rendered atom positions. For polyethylene only, `src/polymer-growth-plan.js` creates a deterministic, bounded continuation whose first points are those exact backbone coordinates. `src/reaction-lab-polymer-cinematic.js` renders one reusable tube over the finite backbone and extends that same tube from its active tangent. The plan module has no DOM, Three.js, Feed, inventory, persistence or chemistry dependency.

Presentation-only ethene units approach the live growth tip. The first three remain atomistic and bond into the continuous backbone; later units use a camera-distance-driven molecular / compact marker LOD. A fixed two-unit atomistic window stays near the moving tip. As the tip advances, atoms and bonds from the oldest part of the actual finite fragment crossfade into the shared coarse backbone. No Stage B bodies, actual molecule instances, Feed slots, resource counts or `PolymerSample` evidence are added or changed.

The chain plan is seeded by the stable sample id. Persistent, bounded curvature produces one calm irregular chain. It keeps the chemistry-derived tangent for its first bonds, then uses the viewer's screen-plane basis to ease any depth-directed travel into a legible view without a direction pop. The plan stays DOM / Three.js independent and receives only three numeric basis vectors. Its maximum is 30 presentation-only repeat units beyond the finite sample, with a 32-point initial-backbone limit, 92 total points, five tube sides and a fixed six-unit feed pool. The renderer has six objects total; it reuses a single indexed tube, instanced atom / bond buffers, a compact-feed pool and one growth-tip marker. Normal and reduced-motion clocks only change duration. Hidden tabs and closed dialogs stop the existing Lab tick; close, purge, next Feed and disposal release the temporary visualization and restore the finite sample's opacity, original depth-write state and camera distance.

During growth, FEED, rack editing, molecule dragging and environment controls are locked for the bounded cinematic. The camera remains fixed until projected chain bounds approach the largest safe rectangle computed from the live canvas, equipment and purge-outlet bounds. It then eases outward only as needed; it does not follow the chain tip or orbit. The cinematic ends at a short long-chain observation hold and then returns to the existing finite Sample Bay / discovery lifecycle. No morphology, multiple chains or material-structure bridge is presented.

`tests/polymer-growth-plan.test.mjs` checks backbone identity, continuation orientation, deterministic curvature, projected long-chain length, timing, UI-aware screen bounds and actual Three.js object / geometry / vertex stability. `tests/reaction-lab-polymer-browser.test.mjs` drives the production Stage B PE route with actual pointer gestures at 390×844 and 1280×900, captures anchor / incorporation / scale-out / hold screenshots, exercises reduced motion and cleanup, and verifies the existing polymer chemistry/discovery routes remain compatible. `scripts/summarize-polymer-growth-profile.mjs` reports update, CPU render-submission and frame-interval p50 / p95 / peak from the same production browser probe; clock values are evidence, not brittle CI limits.
